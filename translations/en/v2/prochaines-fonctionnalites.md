# V2 features

V2 extends the CUBIT market with three uses: depositing CUBIT for a daily reward in CUBIT, observing the market and launching new tokens on a public launchpad.

Momentum and the Forge have been open since September 23, 2026, the Vault since September 26, 2026.

## Opening the features

| Feature | Opening | Planned use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mCUBIT Vault | On September 26, 2026 | CUBIT deposits, 24-hour lock, CUBIT reward paid from a reserve |
| Momentum | On September 23, 2026 | Read-only app page: active walls, partially consumed walls and history of crossed walls |
| Forge | On September 23, 2026, when the launchpad was added | Public launchpad: any account launches an isolated child market by paying the launch fee of 0.005 ETH |

Replacing a module closes its feature until the team reopens it.

## Rewarding deposits

The [Vault](vault.md) offers a nontransferable deposit position, a 24-hour lock and a CUBIT reward of 3% of the deposit per day, to be claimed every day. It is paid only from a reserve: the 20% of the supply allocated at launch, then the CUBIT from fully crossed walls. There are no WETH rewards.

## Observing and creating

[Momentum and Forge](momentum-forge.md) cover two distinct functions: observing the market and launching child markets. Momentum remains read-only. The Forge is a public launchpad, added after the CUBIT launch; its launch fees, in ETH, and tokens absorbed by the children’s walls join the launchpad governance vault.

## Rights associated with modules

The team address can replace the peripheral modules of the registry at any time, without delay, and then activate them. These powers are permanent.

Replacement does not take the funds of an old Vault position or move the reserves of children already created. Each module offered to users must be identified and verified. [Permissions and replacements](../securite/permissions.md).

The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) details the criteria associated with these releases.
